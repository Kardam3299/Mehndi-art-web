Deep Burgundy Color Guide

Rupali's Dark Stain Henna Secrets

Organic henna needs natural warmth to oxidize into a deep, long-lasting mahogany color for your wedding.

1
Keep It On For 6-8 Hrs

Let the henna dry naturally. Keep the paste on your skin for a minimum of 6 hours, or overnight for the deepest stain.

2
Lemon & Sugar Mist

Dab warm lemon and sugar syrup with cotton once semi-dry to keep the herbal henna moist and active on the skin.

3
Clove Fumes (Laung Dhuni)

Warm your hands over gentle pan fumes of roasted cloves. The natural heat accelerates henna oxidation intensely.

4
No Water for 24 Hours

Scrape off the dry paste gently using mustard or coconut oil. Avoid soap and direct water exposure for the first 24 hours.
